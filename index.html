01THE IDEA, IN A MINUTE

Two worlds. One carrier.

A liposome is a small vesicle made from one or more phospholipid bilayers. Its water-filled core and lipid membrane create distinct spaces for different kinds of cargo.

A / 01H₂O

Aqueous core

A water-rich inner space that can entrap suitable hydrophilic molecules.

↗
B / 02

Lipid bilayer

A hydrophobic region between the two aqueous environments, often a location for lipophilic cargo.

↗
C / 03◌

A tunable design

Composition, size and surface properties can be tailored for a specific formulation.

↗

HISTORY

How the idea grew

  1. 1965Alec Bangham describes phospholipid vesicles. Liposomes enter the scientific record.
  2. 1970sResearchers explore vesicles as carriers for drugs, enzymes and antigens.
  3. 1980sSurface coatings such as PEG are studied to slow immune clearance — the “stealth” idea.
  4. 1995Doxil, a pegylated liposomal doxorubicin, becomes the first FDA-approved liposomal medicine.
  5. TodayTargeted, triggered-release and theranostic designs are studied. Each product still needs its own evidence.

COMPARE

Free drug versus a liposomal carrier

Feature Conventional free drug Liposomal delivery
Distribution Spreads according to the drug’s own properties The carrier can change where and how long the drug travels
Exposure Healthy tissues may see the free molecule early The bilayer can shield cargo until release
Cargo space Limited by the drug’s own solubility Aqueous core and lipid bilayer offer two different spaces
Release Often immediate once the dose is given Can be slower or triggered — only if the formulation is designed that way

iA liposome is a delivery platform—not a drug by itself. Performance depends on the formulation, medicine and route of administration.

05VIRTUAL PREPARATION LAB

How are vesicles made?

Compare five widely discussed preparation approaches. The sequence below is a high-level learning map—not a bench protocol.

LIPID FILM → HYDRATION → VESICLES

METHOD 01 / FILM FORMATION

Thin-film hydration

Lipids are first arranged as a thin film. Contact with an aqueous phase allows the lipids to self-assemble into vesicles; further processing may be used to adjust size or lamellarity.

LEARNING SEQUENCE

    WHY IT'S USED

    A foundational approach that makes the self-assembly concept easy to visualize.

    KEEP IN MIND

    The resulting population may be heterogeneous and can require additional size-control steps.

    07LIPOSOMES IN MEDICINE

    From concept to clinic.

    Liposomal formulations are used in several therapeutic areas. Open a card for a high-level formulation note.

    DONCOLOGY

    DOXIL®

    Liposomal doxorubicin

    A pegylated liposomal formulation of doxorubicin.

    Formulation note+

    A medicine carried in a liposomal formulation can have distribution and pharmacokinetic properties that differ from the conventional drug. Refer to the current product label for approved uses and precautions.

    AANTIFUNGAL

    AmBisome®

    Liposomal amphotericin B

    An amphotericin B formulation in liposomes.

    Formulation note+

    The lipid formulation changes how the active ingredient is presented and distributed. It is a distinct product—not interchangeable milligram-for-milligram with every amphotericin formulation.

    OONCOLOGY

    ONIVYDE®

    Liposomal irinotecan

    An irinotecan liposome injection used in specific oncology regimens.

    Formulation note+

    The liposomal carrier is part of the product identity and clinical regimen. Indications and combinations are label-specific; check current prescribing information.

    ELOCAL ANALGESIA

    EXPAREL®

    Liposomal bupivacaine

    A liposomal injectable suspension of the local anesthetic bupivacaine.

    Formulation note+

    This is a specific extended-release product with its own approved administration instructions. The formulation should not be treated as equivalent to other bupivacaine products.

    iBrand examples are for learning only. This page is not a prescribing guide; product indications and warnings can change. Always use the current official label.
